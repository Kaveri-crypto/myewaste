Introduction & Purpose

In the modern era, technology has become an essential part of everyday life. Devices such as mobile phones, computers, and household appliances have improved communication, education, and comfort.

However, the increasing use of these devices has also created a major concern in the form of electronic waste (e-waste). E-waste refers to discarded electronic products that are no longer useful. Unlike ordinary waste, it contains harmful substances such as lead, mercury, and cadmium.

The purpose of this program is to bridge the knowledge gap regarding responsible e-waste management.

Program Objectives

Key goals identified in the Field Project

Education First

Educational institutions play a key role in spreading awareness about the hazards of e-waste.

Recycling Habits

Promoting proper disposal and recycling habits among households and communities.

Sustainability

Guiding better policies for a cleaner tomorrow and sustainable digital practices.

Scope of Awareness

Comprehensive approach to address e-waste challenges.

Target Audience

Primary focus on students and educators. Secondary reach includes households and local shop owners.

Key Areas

Environmental Impact, Health Hazards, Data Security, and Legal Regulations.

Methodology

Structured surveys, observational studies, and government policy reviews.

Stakeholders

Local schools, NGOs, and municipal corporations for ground-level implementation.
