References & Sources

All data and information are verified from official government and international reports.

1. Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)

Official guidelines and implementation status of E-Waste Management Rules, 2016 in India.

https://cpcb.nic.in/e-waste/

2. Global E-Waste Monitor 2020

United Nations University (UNU) report providing global statistics on e-waste generation and recycling rates.

https://globalewaste.org/

3. Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC)

E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2016 Official Notification PDF.

http://moef.gov.in/content/e-waste

4. Toxics Link

Indian environmental research organization providing ground-level reports on e-waste handling.

https://toxicslink.org/

5. Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB)

State-specific guidelines for authorized recyclers and collection centers.

https://mpcb.gov.in/
