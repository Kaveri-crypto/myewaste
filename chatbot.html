Frequently Asked Questions

Top 10 common queries answered

01. What exactly is E-Waste?

+

E-waste refers to any discarded electrical or electronic equipment. This includes computers, mobile phones, televisions, printers, and even small appliances like toasters that are no longer functional or wanted.

02. Why is E-Waste dangerous?

+

E-waste contains toxic heavy metals like Lead, Mercury, Cadmium, and Brominated Flame Retardants. If not disposed of properly, these can leach into soil and water, causing serious health issues.

03. How should I dispose of old phones?

+

Back up your data, perform a factory reset, and take it to an authorized e-waste collection center or a manufacturer take-back program.

04. Can I throw batteries in the regular trash?

+

No. Batteries contain corrosive acids and heavy metals. They should be separated and dropped at specific battery recycling bins.

05. Is selling to a local scrap dealer safe?

+

It is not recommended. Informal scrap dealers often burn wires or use acid, releasing toxic fumes. Always use authorized recyclers.

06. How do I wipe data before recycling?

+

For phones, do a Factory Reset. For computers, use secure erasure software like DBAN or Blancco. Physically destroying hard drives is also effective.

07. What are the benefits of recycling?

+

Recycling recovers valuable materials like Gold, Copper, and Plastic, reducing the need for mining and preventing pollution.

08. Are there laws for E-Waste in India?

+

Yes. The E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2016 mandate proper disposal and make manufacturers responsible under Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR).

09. What happens after I recycle?

+

Authorized recyclers dismantle devices, shred them, and separate plastics, glass, and metals for reuse in new products.

10. How can I organize a collection drive?

+

Partner with a registered recycler (like Karo Sambhav), set up bins in your society, and spread awareness. The recycler will pick up the waste.
