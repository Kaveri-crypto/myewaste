Awareness Tips

Practical guides for responsible e-waste management.

Do's & Don'ts

Recommended Actions

1
Donate Working Devices

Give old phones/laptops to charities.

2
Use Certified Recyclers

Check CPCB list for authorized centers.

3
Wipe Your Data

Factory reset devices before disposal.

Prohibited Actions

1
No Regular Bins

Never throw electronics in household trash.

2
Don't Burn Wires

Burning releases deadly toxic fumes.

3
Avoid Scrap Dealers

Unauthorized dealers lack safety standards.

Safety Practices

For Handlers

Wear insulated gloves and masks. Ensure ventilation.

Community Role

Organize regular collection drives.

Battery Safety

Do not crush Li-ion batteries. Store in non-flammable containers.

Data Security

Physically destroy hard drives for security.

Real-Life Case Study

The Transformation of Seelampur, Delhi

Location: Seelampur, Delhi.
The Problem: Informal processing, burning cables, severe health issues.
The Intervention: E-Waste Rules 2016 & NGO training.
The Impact: Transitioning to formalized, safer dismantling clusters.
